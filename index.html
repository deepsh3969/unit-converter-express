          About

          Unit Converter Express is a fast browser-based unit conversion tool designed for students, engineers, developers and everyday calculations.

          Every calculation runs locally inside your browser using a centralised conversion registry. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and the application keeps working offline once it has loaded.
